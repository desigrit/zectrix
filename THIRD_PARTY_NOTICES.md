# Third-party notices

## Carlito

Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/carlito/tree/3a810cab78ebd6e2e4eed42af9e8453c4f9b850a/fonts/ttf.

The calendar image uses the unmodified Carlito Regular and Bold TrueType fonts by the Carlito Project Authors. Carlito was designed by Lukasz Dziedzic as a metric-compatible alternative to Calibri. These fonts are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete notice is in `note_calendar/assets/fonts/Carlito-LICENSE.txt`.

## Tamzen bitmap fonts

Source: https://github.com/sunaku/tamzen-font, commit `3255e8259bc9b880c60ab8b737ec8aa574e00d75`.

The fonts and generated firmware setup-screen glyph table are derived from Tamzen by Suraj N. Kurapati, based on Tamsyn by Scott Fial. The complete notices and permission terms are in `note_calendar/assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`.

## NOTE4C display initialization and hardware pin mapping

The SSD2683 four-color command sequence follows the public reference implementation at https://github.com/LazyYoun/youn-ink-fourcolor-firmware/tree/2bp, especially `custom_lcd_display.cc` and the Note 4c board definitions. The controller and calendar application are separate original code.
